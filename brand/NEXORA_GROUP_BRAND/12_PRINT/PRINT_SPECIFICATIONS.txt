NEXORA GROUP Print Specifications
Master vector should be preferred for all professional production.
CMYK PDF files are generated through DeviceCMYK conversion and should be preflighted by the selected print provider against their ICC workflow.
Raster proof files: 300 and 600 DPI-equivalent at A4-class width.
Pantone: no physical Pantone assignment is certified in this package. Select/approve using a physical Pantone guide before industrial production.
Bleed: application-specific; 3 mm is a common starting point for stationery but printer specifications govern.
